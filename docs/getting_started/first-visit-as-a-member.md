So you've attended an open evening, signed up and perhaps even got your key fob, now what do you need to know?

## Light Switches

Most of our lights need turning on and off manually. Nearly all the light switches are located on the left as you walk through the inner doors. The only exception is Snackspace, for which the switches are inside Snackspace, between the two sets of doors.

## Toilets
We have two toilets. 'The Executive Bathroom' is located immediately to the left as you enter the space. This is accessible, has a sanitary bin, and is also where most of our cleaning consumables are stored.

The other toilet is located at the other end of Hackspace, opposite the 'messy sink'.
